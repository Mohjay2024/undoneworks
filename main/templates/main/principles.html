10 Principles - Das Niyam

The foundation of Arya Samaj - given by Maharishi Dayanand Saraswati

1. God is the source

God is the source of all true knowledge and all that is known through knowledge.

2. God's Nature

God is existent, intelligent, blissful, formless, almighty, just, merciful, unborn, infinite, unchangeable and holy.

3. Vedas

Vedas are the scriptures of true knowledge. It is the duty of all Aryas to read, teach and hear them.

4. Truth

We should always be ready to accept truth and reject falsehood.

5. Dharma

All actions should be done according to Dharma after considering right and wrong.

6. Welfare of World

The primary aim of Arya Samaj is to do good to the whole world - physical, spiritual and social progress.

7. Love and Justice

Treat all with love, justice and according to their merit.

8. Knowledge

We should promote knowledge and dispel ignorance.

9. Selflessness

No one should be satisfied with his own welfare, but should see his welfare in the welfare of others.

10. Freedom

All should follow social rules for everyone's welfare, but everyone is free in personal matters that benefit them.
