A Century of Vedic Service in Kenya

Arya Samaj Nairobi was established in 1903 by the early Indian community in Kenya, inspired by the vision of Maharishi Dayanand Saraswati (1824-1883) and his call - Krinvanto Vishwam Aryam - Make the world noble.
