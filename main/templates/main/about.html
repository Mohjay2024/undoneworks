About Arya Samaj

Founded by Maharishi Dayanand Saraswati in 1875 in Bombay, India. Arya Samaj is a Vedic reform movement. Its motto is "Om Krinvanto Vishwam Aryam"

Maharishi Dayanand Saraswati (1824-1883)

A sannyasi, scholar and social reformer. He wrote Satyarth Prakash (The Light of Truth). He taught that truth alone should triumph and that the Vedas are the source of all true knowledge.

Arya Samaj Nairobi

Established in 1903 by the Indian community in Kenya and nestled in Parklands, Nairobi, is a Vedic spiritual home founded on the teachings of Maharishi Dayanand Saraswati and his call “Back to the Vedas.” rooted in belief in One formlesssupreme being, the wisdom of the Vedas, and the oneness of humanity, we welcome all seekers without distinction of caste or creed, with daily havans for peace and reflection and our main Sunday Satsang at 11:00 AM featuring discourse, bhajans, and community fellowship

Our Mission

To make the world noble - Krinvanto Vishwam Aryam. To promote Vedic knowledge and serve humanity.

Our Values

Truth, Education, Equality and Service to all.
